## Company registration

Register your Private Limited company in **7 to 14 working days**, in three simple steps:

1. **Name your company.** We check availability and reserve the best option.
2. **Submit your NIC.** For most companies, NIC copies and a few details are all we need.
3. **Receive your certificate.** We file with the Registrar of Companies and deliver your Certificate of Incorporation.

Registration is offered in three packages (**Essential**, **Business** and **Complete**), from a straightforward registration to a full launch with tax registration, bookkeeping and your first online presence. There are no hidden fees, and government fees are always quoted upfront.
